# Rare Bird Records — New Online System

We're proposing to move the rare-bird-record process off email and onto a simple website. The people, the rules, and the final decisions stay the same as today — what changes is that the whole thing runs through one organised system instead of a chain of emails, with nothing lost along the way and no email required to use it.

## Roles and permissions

| Role | Who | Logs in? | Can do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Observer** | Anyone reporting a sighting | No — identified only by a private link they get after submitting | Submit a new record; add more information later if the secretary asks; nothing else |
| **Reviewer** | The panel's named members | Yes — username/password set up by the secretary | See records open for review; record and update their own decision and comment; see other reviewers' votes only once the secretary reveals them |
| **Secretary** | Runs the process; does not vote | Yes | Everything a reviewer can see, plus: check a submission is complete, move a record through each stage, reveal votes for open discussion, record the final decision |

## The two forms

| | Submission form | Review form |
|---|---|---|
| **Used by** | Observer | Reviewer |
| **Access** | Open to anyone, no account | Requires login |
| **Captures** | Species, written description, location, date, optional photos/sound recordings | A decision — Accept, Reject, Abstain, Pend, or Own Record — plus a written comment |
| **When it's used** | At submission, and again later via the private link if more detail is requested | Once the secretary has opened the record for review; can be updated any time before the final decision |

Both forms feed the same underlying record for that sighting — there's one entry per sighting, not a separate copy per form.

## The process, step by step

| Step | What happens | Who's responsible |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Submit | Observer reports the sighting through the submission form | Observer |
| 2. Check | Secretary confirms the record is complete, or requests more detail | Secretary |
| 3. Private review | Each reviewer records a decision and comment, without seeing anyone else's yet | Reviewers |
| 4. Open discussion | Secretary reveals all votes and comments; reviewers may revise theirs, and anyone who Pended is expected to settle on Accept or Reject | Reviewers, prompted by Secretary |
| 5. Final decision | Secretary applies the committee's existing acceptance thresholds and records the outcome | Secretary |

## Where the information lives

Everything sits in one secure database rather than scattered emails — a single entry per sighting that always shows its current status and latest information. Separately, every change, vote, and comment is permanently logged with who did it and when, so the full history is always available even though day to day people only see the current state. There's no automatic emailing in this first version: reviewers and the secretary simply check the site for what's waiting on them, and that list is itself the notification.

## What's the same as today

The committee still doesn't identify birds for anyone — the observer states the species and makes the case. The same five decision categories, the same acceptance thresholds, and the non-voting secretary role all carry over unchanged. This is a new tool for an existing process, not a new process.
